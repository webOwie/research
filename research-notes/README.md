# Research Notes

Research Notes are working records for technical observations, experiments, architecture decisions and early findings that are useful to preserve publicly but are not formal publications.

## Current status

No individual research note is indexed here yet.

## Minimum metadata for future notes

- title
- date
- author
- status: draft, active, superseded or archived
- question or observation
- context and assumptions
- evidence or source references
- method used
- result or current interpretation
- limitations and unresolved questions
- related project or publication

Research Notes must remain clearly labeled as working material so that preliminary observations are not confused with peer-reviewed or formally published results.
