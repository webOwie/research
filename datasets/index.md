# Datasets

This index is reserved for datasets appropriate for public research use with documented provenance, scope and licensing.

## Minimum metadata for future entries

- dataset title and purpose
- creator or maintainer
- creation and update dates
- source provenance
- collection methodology
- coverage and known limitations
- formats and schema
- privacy and ethical considerations where relevant
- license
- persistent identifier or stable download URL
- checksum or version information where appropriate
