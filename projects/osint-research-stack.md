# OSINT Research Stack

**Status:** Active R&D  
**Area:** OSINT & Information Intelligence · Research Automation

## Purpose

The OSINT Research Stack explores a modular environment for search, acquisition, verification, analysis and documentation of publicly available information while preserving source traceability and explicit operator control.

## Research questions

- How can heterogeneous public data sources be normalized without obscuring provenance?
- How can automated collection remain auditable and reproducible?
- How can research workflows separate discovery, verification, analysis and reporting?
- How can local AI assist analysis without becoming an unverifiable source of truth?

## Current scope

- federated search and source routing
- OSINT-oriented data acquisition
- source-quality and provenance metadata
- reproducible investigation workflows
- local AI-assisted analysis
- evidence-oriented exports and documentation
- modular integrations for research tooling

## Boundaries

The research focus is on lawful collection and analysis of publicly available or otherwise authorized information. The project record does not imply access to private accounts, restricted communications or unauthorized systems.

## Output policy

Stable tools, methods, datasets or technical reports will be indexed only when a public source record exists.
